Inline mini-game arcade that appears below the live reasoning module in the DSH chat flow. 7 games (Breakout, Snake, Reflex, 2048, Memory, Stack, Flappy), auto-pauses/fades when the answer starts, optional continue entry, random/pinned game, best scores, optional WebAudio sound, no runtime dependencies. Tested on DSH 0.2.2-rc.1. MIT.
